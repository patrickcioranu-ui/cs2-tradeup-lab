MODEL NOTES

Transparent by design.

Collection selection is weighted by the number of inputs from each collection; outputs inside a collection are equally likely. Output floats use CS2's weighted-float rule. Prices are the lowest current CSFloat listings (via prices.csgotrader.app), converted to euros at the ECB daily rate and refreshed daily. A listing is not a guaranteed sale price: "thin market" means there is no buy order near the listing, so selling at that price may take a long time. Spreads, volatility, trade locks and fees can change the result.

Expected value EV = Σ P(output) × net sale price − input cost
